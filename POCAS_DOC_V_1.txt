Post Office Agency Collection Management System – Project Documentation
Post Office customer account service (POCAS)
1. Introduction

This document describes the functional and technical design of the Post Office Agency Collection Management System (PACMS). The system is intended to digitize and manage customer savings accounts, daily/monthly collections, and passbook-like tracking for a post office agency.

The application will be used by Admin (Agency Owner), Collection Persons, and Customers, with clearly separated responsibilities and access levels.

The document is designed to be future-proof, allowing easy addition of new features such as online payments, QR scanning, and extended reports.

2. Problem Statement

Currently, customer savings, daily collections, and passbook entries are managed manually, which leads to:

Human errors in calculation

Difficulty in tracking daily/monthly collections

No real-time visibility for customers

No centralized system for reporting and auditing

PACMS aims to solve these problems through a secure, scalable digital system.

3. Objectives

Digitally manage customer post office savings accounts

Track daily and monthly collections accurately

Provide passbook-style visibility to customers

Enable role-based access (Admin, Collector, Customer)

Support long-term savings plans (3-year / 5-year)

Allow future expansion (online payments, QR scan, reports)

4. User Roles & Access Control
4.1 Admin (Agency Owner)

Responsibilities:

Register and manage customers

Open and manage post office accounts

Assign collectors

Define monthly installment (Kist)

View and edit all collections

Manage account lifecycle (Open / Running / Closed)

Generate reports

Access Level: Full access

4.2 Collection Person (Field Collector)

Responsibilities:

Collect money on a daily basis from customers

Update daily collection entries

View assigned customers only

View monthly total collection summary

Restrictions:

Cannot edit customer master details

Cannot access other collectors' data

4.3 Customer

Responsibilities:

View own account details

View passbook entries

Track paid installments

View remaining duration

Access Level: Read-only (own data only)

Authentication will be based on Account Number + Mobile Number / OTP.

5. Core Modules
5.1 Customer Management Module

Stores and manages customer master data.

Customer Details:

Customer Name

Account Number (Unique)

Mobile Number

Address (Optional)

Account Type (3 Years / 5 Years)

Monthly Installment Amount

Account Opening Date

Account Closing Date

Account Status (Active / Completed / Closed)

5.2 Account Management Module

Handles post office savings account lifecycle.

Features:

Automatic calculation of account closing date

Remaining months calculation

Total expected deposit calculation

Completion status tracking

5.3 Collection Management Module (Daily Collection)

Used exclusively by Collection Persons.

Key Features:

Calendar-based daily entry system

Date-wise money entry

Support for partial payments

Monthly auto-sum calculation

Flow:

Collector selects customer

Selects date from calendar

Enters collected amount

System stores date-wise entry

At month-end, system calculates total

5.4 Monthly Posting Module

Maps daily collections to monthly post office deposits.

Logic:

Sum of daily collections = Monthly contribution

Monthly contribution added to customer account

Passbook updated automatically

5.5 Passbook Module (Customer View)

Digital version of a physical passbook.

Displayed Information:

Month & Year

Monthly installment amount

Paid amount

Pending amount (if any)

Total balance till date

Customers can only view, not edit.

5.6 Reports & Dashboard Module

Admin Reports:

Daily collection summary

Monthly collection report

Customer-wise account status

Collector-wise performance

Collector View:

Daily total collection

Monthly total collection

6. Future Enhancements (Planned)
6.1 Online Payment Integration

Customers can pay directly via UPI / Net Banking

Payments automatically mapped to their account

Reduced dependency on manual collection

6.2 QR Code / Scanner-Based Payment

Each customer profile will have a unique QR code

Collector or customer can scan and pay

Payment linked automatically to customer account

6.3 Notification System

Monthly payment reminders

Account completion notifications

Payment confirmation alerts

6.4 Audit & Compliance

Complete transaction history

Read-only audit logs

Export data for regulatory compliance
