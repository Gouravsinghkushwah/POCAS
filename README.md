POCAS helps a post office customer account service their saving accounts, and daily/monthly money collection in one system instead of registers and passbooks.

More simply:
Admin adds customers
Customers have saving accounts (3-year / 5-year plans)
Money collected daily is recorded
Monthly totals are calculated automatically
Customers can see a digital passbook
Reports are generated for tracking and auditing

Result:
✔ Less manual work
✔ No calculation errors
✔ Clear tracking of money
✔ Easy future upgrades (online payment, QR, reports)
